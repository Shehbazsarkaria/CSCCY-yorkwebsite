01 // About

Where security meets the cloud.

We're a student-run community at York University for anyone curious about offensive security, defensive engineering, cloud infrastructure, and everything in between. No prior experience required — just bring the curiosity.

Security First

Hands-on labs, CTF practice, and deep dives into real-world vulnerabilities and defence.

Cloud Native

Containers, CI/CD, IAM, and deployment pipelines. Learn the stack that runs the internet.

Workshops & Talks

Beginner-friendly workshops, alumni panels, and industry speaker nights throughout the term.

Community

A network of students who collaborate on projects, prep for interviews, and help each other grow.

02 // What We Do

Build. Break. Deploy. Repeat.

From capture-the-flag weekends to cloud certification study groups — here's a taste of what the club gets up to.

CTF Team

Compete in student CTFs or just hang out and solve challenges with the team.

Cloud Labs

Spin up real infrastructure — VMs, containers, serverless — in guided lab sessions.

Project Teams

Collaborate on open-source tools, home labs, and club infrastructure projects.

Study Groups

Cert prep and course support for security, networking, and cloud fundamentals.
