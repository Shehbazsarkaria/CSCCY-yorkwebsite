About us

We're a student-run club built around one idea: you learn security and cloud faster when you're curious and have people to try things with. Beginners and experienced members share the same sessions.

Security
Hands-on labs, CTF practice, and walkthroughs of real vulnerabilities and defences.
Cloud
Containers, pipelines, access control, and deploying things that stay up.
Workshops
Beginner-friendly sessions and speaker nights through the term.
People
Project partners, study groups, and interview prep with other York students.
